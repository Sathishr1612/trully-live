Luxury on the Edge of the Wild

The forest begins where the road ends — and that’s where you’ll find Trulyy The Gir Lion Safari Camp. Set amidst mango orchards, open landscapes, and along the tranquil riverfront on the edge of Gir National Park, this is an intimate wilderness escape where adventure begins at your doorstep. Stay in 10 luxury tents, 11 luxury cottages, or 4 jungle suites, thoughtfully designed to immerse you in nature without compromising on comfort. Days unfold with exhilarating Asiatic lion safaris, guided forest explorations, and peaceful riverfront moments surrounded by Gir’s remarkable wildlife. Evenings slow down beneath open skies, surrounded by silence, stars, and the untamed beauty of the forest. And for intimate private gatherings, few settings are as memorable — because sometimes, nature needs no decoration.
