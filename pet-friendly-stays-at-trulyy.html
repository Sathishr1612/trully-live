Pet-Friendly Stays at Trulyy

At Trulyy India Hotels, we believe memorable journeys are best shared—with every member of the family. Select Trulyy properties are pet-friendly, welcoming your furry companions to be part of your travel story.

Pet stays are available at select properties only, with prior confirmation.

We welcome well-trained, vaccinated pets.

Pets must always remain under the supervision of their owners.

A pet cleaning or maintenance fee may apply at certain properties.

Pets are not permitted in dining areas, pool zones, or restricted spaces.

Any damage caused by pets will be chargeable to the guest.

Need Assistance?

For specific pet-friendly policies and availability, we recommend contacting our reservations team before confirming your booking.
