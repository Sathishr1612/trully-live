About Us

Trulyy India Hotels & Resorts is a fast-growing Indian hospitality brand built on a simple yet powerful belief — every journey should feel personal, immersive, and truly memorable. With an expanding collection of boutique hotels, luxury wildlife lodges, heritage retreats, and contemporary resorts, we bring travellers closer to India’s culture, nature, and warmth through thoughtfully designed experiences.

Inspired Hospitality

Rooted in Indian values and driven by modern hospitality standards, Trulyy India has become a preferred choice for families, leisure travellers, wildlife enthusiasts, wedding planners, and corporates seeking immersive escapes across India.

Our Promise

Trulyy India is more than a hospitality brand — it is a gateway to India’s culture, wilderness, warmth, and wonder. Stay with us to experience stories, landscapes, emotions, and moments that truly stay with you.

To create authentic and personalized travel experiences that connect guests with the rich culture, heritage, and natural beauty of India, while delivering exceptional hospitality and sustainable practices.
