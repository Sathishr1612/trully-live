Heritage Soul, Business-Ready Heart

In the vibrant center of Udaipur, The Amar Mahal marries heritage charm with contemporary function. 60 well-appointed rooms — Executive, Super Deluxe, and Royal Suite categories — offer elegant interiors and layouts that work for short stays and longer engagements alike.

Central location means you’re never far from the action. Flexible spaces suit small gatherings and corporate interactions. For business travellers who want character with their comfort, or teams seeking a curated base in the City of Lakes, Amar Mahal delivers seamless stays with a sense of place.
