Revenue & Distribution

Revenue Manager – OTA & Distribution

📍 Ahmedabad / Corporate Full-time 3–6+ yrs exp.

We are seeking a dynamic Revenue Manager to drive revenue growth, optimize online distribution, and manage OTA performance across the portfolio of Trulyy India Hotels, Resorts & Camps. The role focuses on pricing strategy, channel management, inventory optimization, demand forecasting, and maximizing RevPAR through strategic distribution management.

Revenue Management
  • Develop and implement pricing & revenue strategies to maximize Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR
  • Monitor daily pick-up, pace reports, forecasts and competitor pricing
  • Manage dynamic pricing based on seasonality, demand patterns and market trends
  • Prepare monthly revenue forecasts and performance analysis reports
  • Identify upselling, cross-selling and ancillary revenue opportunities
OTA & Distribution
  • Manage all OTA platforms: Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda and channel partners
  • Optimize listings, content, promotions, visibility and conversion
  • Manage rate parity, inventory allocation and distribution strategy
  • Oversee channel manager connectivity and CRS updates
  • Negotiate OTA partnerships, promotions and campaign participation
Performance & Analytics
  • Analyze channel production, booking trends, market share and competitor benchmarking
  • Monitor OTA commissions, acquisition costs and channel profitability
  • Generate MIS reports and actionable insights for management
  • Track KPIs including Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, channel mix and conversion metrics
Strategy & Collaboration
  • Partner with sales, reservations, marketing and property teams on demand generation
  • Support launch and revenue setup for new properties
  • Drive direct booking growth while optimizing third-party channel contribution
  • Recommend distribution expansion and market penetration strategies
Requirements
  • 3–6+ years experience in hotel revenue management and OTA distribution
  • Strong knowledge of channel managers, PMS, CRS and revenue management tools
  • Experience handling multi-property hospitality portfolios preferred
  • Strong analytical, negotiation and advanced Excel/reporting capabilities
  • Understanding of Indian leisure and destination hospitality markets preferred
Key KPIs
RevPAR Growth ADR Optimization Occupancy Growth OTA Profitability Channel Mix Rate Parity Direct Booking
