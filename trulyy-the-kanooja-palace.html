Where Royal Rajasthan Meets Modern Celebration

Imagine waking to the Aravalli hills rising beyond a tranquil lakeside setting, inside a palace where Rajasthan’s grandeur is not simply admired but lived. Trulyy The Kanooja Palace brings together timeless Rajasthani architecture and contemporary comfort across 47 well-appointed rooms, 13 suites, and 10 upcoming rooms. Sweeping lawns, elegant archways, expansive event spaces, and the stillness of the lake create a setting made for moments that deserve to be remembered.

Created for destination weddings, multi-day celebrations, and private gatherings, Trulyy The Kanooja Palace gives every occasion its own sense of grandeur. From an intimate mehendi beside the lake to a grand sangeet beneath the stars, celebrations unfold against a landscape where palace, water, and Aravalli hills come together naturally — creating a backdrop that needs very little embellishment.
